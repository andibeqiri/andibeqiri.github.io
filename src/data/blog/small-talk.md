I have never been a fan of typical small talk questions:

“How are you?” “Where are you from?” “What do you do?”

They feel like interrogation—questions that try to fit a person to their current state, where they were born, or what they have chosen to do with their life.

I think the best small talk is the kind that tries to actually get to know the other person through their feelings, motivations, and experiences.

This approach is so much harder to do well, though the depth of connection makes the effort worthwhile…
